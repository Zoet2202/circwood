Wood flows in Norway

A national material- and carbon-flow analysis of Norwegian wood — Circwood WP4, Norwegian Institute of Wood Technology (NTI).

Nodes: all

Regional data exists for the harvest edges only — SSB publishes no regional mill data, so a county view shows what that county contributes to the national species pools. Everything downstream is attributed nationally. “National (not regionally attributed)” carries the firewood estimate SSB never regionalises.

Destination applies to the export edges only (SSB 08799, trade by partner country). A partner view shows the share of each export flow that went to that country; the netted raw-wood and sawn-timber exports are attributed by the gross export mix, since no partner-level net figure exists. “Domestic” is every flow that stays in Norway.
